# Product Vision

**MarkGauge** — working name for discovery  
**Document type:** Vision and elevator pitch  
**Status:** Draft for validation

---

## Elevator Pitch

MarkGauge is a personal market monitoring workspace for individual investors who want live equity data, a watchlist they own, and alerts that fire when prices cross the lines they draw — without opening a brokerage account or drowning in generic finance feeds. One login, one dashboard, one notification channel when it matters.

---

## Name Rationale

**MarkGauge** pairs *market* with *gauge*: a focused instrument that shows what you care about, not every ticker on the exchange. The name signals measurement and clarity rather than trading, social hype, or professional-terminal complexity. It is memorable, pronounceable, and available for branding without tying the product to a single broker or data vendor.

---

## Vision Statement

We believe individual investors deserve the same calm, consolidated visibility that institutions arrange with expensive tooling — delivered as a lightweight web product that respects their time, their symbol list, and their alert rules.

MarkGauge will become the default morning open tab for people who track public equities: scan the market, review their list, read news tied to those names, and trust that they will be notified when conditions they defined are met.

---

## What MarkGauge Is

| Dimension | Position |
|-----------|----------|
| **Category** | Personal equity monitoring and alerting |
| **Primary job** | Help users know what moved, what matters, and when to look again |
| **Delivery** | Modern web application, desktop-first, authenticated per user |
| **Data stance** | Live and near-live quotes from a licensed market data provider; user-owned lists and alert rules stored securely |
| **Intelligence** | Optional AI-assisted news summaries grounded in retrieved content — insight, not trade advice |

---

## What MarkGauge Is Not (at the vision level)

- A brokerage or order execution platform
- A social network for stock tips or sentiment
- A replacement for professional Bloomberg-style terminals
- A robo-advisor or portfolio allocation engine

## Core Promise

> **MarkGauge is your gauge on the market — live data, your list, your alerts, one place.**

Users should feel that opening MarkGauge answers three questions quickly:

1. What is the market doing right now?
2. How are the symbols on my list performing?
3. Am I covered if something hits my price threshold, or do I need to set an alert?

---

## Strategic Themes

### Clarity over noise

Default experience favors a readable dashboard and a curated watchlist, not infinite scroll of unrelated headlines or promotional content.

### User-owned context

Watchlists and alerts belong to the account. The product is built around persistence and recall, not ephemeral browsing.

### Timely awareness

Alerts and email notifications are first-class — the product fails its mission if users learn about a cross hours late because they forgot to refresh.

### Trustworthy data

Quotes and company context come from integrated APIs with explicit error handling; the UI does not guess prices when upstream data is unavailable.

### Room to grow

Vision includes AI summaries and rich charts, but the north star remains *monitoring and notification* before *trading and community*.

---

## Success Picture (12-Month Horizon)

A validated MarkGauge user:

- Checks the dashboard or watchlist at least several times per week during market hours
- Maintains an active watchlist of five or more symbols
- Has at least one price alert configured and has received a notification that matched their rule
- Describes the product as replacing a prior habit (multiple tabs, spreadsheet, or broker-only lists)

We are not optimizing for daily trade volume or assets under management — we are optimizing for reliable attention management.

---

## Alignment with the Problem

The problem statement describes fragmented tools and missed moves. MarkGauge answers that by unifying data, lists, and alerts under one identity and one notification path. Every feature decision in later milestones should be testable against: *does this reduce tab-hopping or improve timely awareness?*
